Dedicated Development Tracks

Official Curriculum & Focused Care Tracks

As officially displayed on our campus, Lingam School specializes in supporting five foundational neurodivergent developmental pathways, led by certified practitioners.

Spectrum Track

Autism Support

Focuses on structured TEACCH methodologies, functional verbal & non-verbal communication, sensory diet calibration, and reducing sensory overload in quiet, safe spaces.

  • Visual Schedule & PECS Integration
  • Sensory Room Desensitization
  • Peer Socialization Groups
Admissions for Autism Program
Developmental Track

Down's Syndrome Support

Emphasizes cognitive enrichment, verbal articulation, hypotonia management through physical therapy, and social-emotional development that encourages natural warmth.

  • Speech & Oral Motor Therapy
  • Muscle Tone Strengthening
  • Early Academic Readiness
Down's Syndrome Curriculum
Physical & Mobility

Cerebral Palsy Therapy

Comprehensive physical rehabilitation, posture correction, gait training, assistive mobility devices, and fine-motor coordination activities for greater physical independence.

  • Daily Physiotherapy Exercises
  • Assistive Posture Support
  • Fine-motor Hand Dexterity
Motor Skill Programs
Life Skills & Academics

Intellectual Disability Care

Tailored functional academics (reading signs, handling currency, telling time) paired with self-help skills: dressing, personal hygiene, and independent task completion.

  • Functional Literacy & Numeracy
  • ADL (Activities of Daily Living)
  • Vocational Training Foundations

About Our Institution

A Compassionate Sanctuary for Growth & Independence

Founded with an unwavering commitment to neurodiverse children, Lingam School for Special Education provides a dignified, warm, and professional learning home. We recognize that no two children develop at the same pace or in the exact same manner.

Our multi-disciplinary team consists of licensed special educators, certified speech and language pathologists, occupational therapists, and clinical psychologists working in unison to create measurable milestones for each learner.

Evidence-Based Care

Validated clinical frameworks tailored to South Indian cultural contexts.

Unconditional Warmth

Every child is welcomed with open arms, patience, and joyful encouragement.
